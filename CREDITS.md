sources are MIT licensed. Where a skill started as someone else's, it is named below.

| --- | --- |

| `type-driven-development` | Principles adapted from [dmmulroy/skills](https://github.com/dmmulroy/skills), [Makisuo/skills](https://github.com/Makisuo/skills), and [kitlangton/skills](https://github.com/kitlangton/skills) |

| `effect-patterns` | Application patterns adapted from [kitlangton/skills](https://github.com/kitlangton/skills), [dmmulroy/skills](https://github.com/dmmulroy/skills), and [Makisuo/skills](https://github.com/Makisuo/skills) |
| `shape-feature` | Original synthesis informed by [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), [dmmulroy/skills](https://github.com/dmmulroy/skills), and [Dax Raad on product restraint](https://kentcdodds.com/chats/07/01/product-sense-restraint-and-opencode-with-dax-raad) |
| `agent-browser` | Unmodified from [vercel-labs/agent-browser](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-browser/tree/main/skills/agent-browser), installed with the skills CLI (see `vendor/skills-lock.json`) |
| `audit-tests` | Adapted in our own words from the authoring gate, junk patterns, and audit of [openclaw's test-audit skill](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/.agents/skills/test-audit/SKILL.md); its writing reference moved from `type-driven-development` |
| `review-diff` | Informed by the deslop, no-comments, and interrogate practices in [poteto's pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) |
| `build` | Tracks and gates informed by the feature, multi-phase-plan, and opening-a-pr playbooks in [poteto's pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack), [HumanLayer's research, plan, implement](https://github.com/humanlayer/advanced-context-engineering-for-coding-agents/blob/main/ace-fca.md), [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) vertical slices, and Kent Beck's tidy first; trunk-based pull requests from [Paul Hammant's trunk-based development](https://trunkbaseddevelopment.com/), [DORA](https://dora.dev/capabilities/trunk-based-development/), and [Google's small CLs](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/developer/small-cls.html); ask and show from [Rouan Wilsenach's Ship / Show / Ask](https://martinfowler.com/articles/ship-show-ask.html); releasable techniques from Martin Fowler's [keystone interface](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/KeystoneInterface.html) and [branch by abstraction](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/BranchByAbstraction.html), Danilo Sato's [parallel change](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/ParallelChange.html), and Pete Hodgson's [feature toggles](https://martinfowler.com/articles/feature-toggles.html) |
| `diagnose` | Informed by the fix-root-causes and attack-the-premise principles in [poteto's pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) and [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) |
| `write-docs` | Informed by the unslop and technical-writing skills in [poteto's pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack), the cache and sediment ideas in `writing-for-agents` from [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/), [Google's timeless documentation](https://developers.google.com/style/timeless-documentation), and [Wikipedia's signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) |
| `design-engineering` | Emil Kowalski's design engineering and [animations.dev](https://animations.dev/) material |

Except for the unmodified `effect-ts` and `agent-browser`, these are maintained here and do not track upstream.

`effect-patterns` adds application-level judgment without copying that manual.
