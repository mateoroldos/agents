# Status block

Fill it from the tracker and the plan, and drop the lines that don't apply.

```text
Epic   #13  SaaS auth                              issue 1 of 5
Issue  #8   Verified identity and tenant-safe …    PR 6 of 8 · 5 merged
PR     6    Organization creation                  design · planning
Next   ⚠️ approve the design
```

- An issue with no epic has no Epic line; an issue with one pull request shows `PR 1 of 1`.
- The PR line gives the approach (`precedent <path>` or `design`) and the stage: `planning`, `approved`, `building`, `in review`, or `merged`.
- Next says what the user must do, marked ⚠️, or what the agent does next.
